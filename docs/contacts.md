# Emergency contacts

Open **Contacts** from the bottom bar. Each card shows the name, number, preferred action and message preview. Disabled contacts are greyed out and marked **Disabled**.

## Add a contact

Tap **Add contact**, fill in the form and tap **Save contact**. **Name** and **Phone number** are required.

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Name** | How the contact appears in the app. |
| **Phone number** | The number to text or call. Include the country code if they're abroad. |
| **Preferred action** | **SMS**, **Call**, or **SMS and call** (default). |
| **Message preview** | Your own words, placed at the top of the automatic alert text. |
| **Notify during SOS** | Turn off to keep the contact saved but leave them out of alerts. |

## The ⋮ menu

Edit
:   Change any field.

Disable for SOS / Enable for SOS
:   Temporarily leave a contact out of alerts without deleting them.

Send test message
:   Opens your SMS app with this contact's message filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send there.

Remove
:   Deletes the contact after you confirm.

## Tips

- Add **at least two** people, in case one can't answer.
- Only one call is placed per SOS. It goes to the **highest contact in the list** set to **Call** or **SMS and call**. New contacts are added to the bottom, so add the person you most want called first. Alternatively, set the contacts above them to **SMS**.
- Tell your contacts you've added them, and what a BY Protect alert looks like. Show them [What your contacts receive](alerts/messages.md).
