# Glossary

Armed
:   Monitoring is running: camera, microphone, motion and location are active and SOS shortcuts work.

Cue
:   One thing the app detected, such as a loud sound or an impact. Cues are combined to decide on an alert.

Event
:   A saved record of an alert or SOS, with its cues, location, video and actions. See [Event History](events/history.md).

Level 1 · Local Deterrent
:   The lowest alert. It is logged and clears after 30 seconds. Nothing is sent.

Level 2 · Silent Alert
:   Texts your contacts silently after a 15-second countdown you can cancel.

Level 3 · Full SOS
:   Immediate alarm, contacts, call, protected video and location. It has no countdown.

Location history
:   Your location, saved every 60 seconds while armed and kept on the phone for 30 days. See [Privacy](privacy.md#location-history).

Protected segments
:   Video clips copied out of the rolling buffer when an event happens, so they aren't overwritten.

Rolling buffer
:   The last few minutes of video, recorded continuously while armed. The oldest clip is overwritten as new ones are recorded. What is left when you stop is cleared the next time you arm.

Trusted contact
:   Someone in your **Contacts** list who is notified during alerts.

## Cues

| Cue | Comes from | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Face presence cue | Camera | A face is close in front of you. |
| Crowding cue | Camera | Several people are around you. On its own this never counts as serious. |
| Rapid approach cue | Camera | Someone is moving towards you quickly. |
| Obstruction cue | Camera | The camera view is blocked, so the app may miss things. It's logged, but never raises an alert. |
| Loud sound cue | Microphone | A sudden loud noise, such as a shout or bang. |
| Bad language cue | Speech monitor | Profanity was heard. |
| Emotional distress cue | Speech monitor | Speech sounded afraid, distressed or angry. |
| Threatening speech cue | Speech monitor | A threat, a demand (for example for your wallet), a command to go somewhere, or a call for help. |
| Walking motion cue | Motion | You're walking. |
| Running or shaking cue | Motion | Running, or the phone being shaken hard. |
| Impact cue | Motion | A sharp jolt or blow. |
| Impact then stillness cue | Motion | An impact followed by sudden stillness, like a fall or being knocked down. This goes straight to a full SOS. |
| Worn position warning | Motion | The phone has been lying face down for a while, so the camera can't see. |
