BY Protect is developed by **BY Technology**.

- :material-email-outline: Email: [support@bytechnology.org](mailto:support@bytechnology.org)
- :material-web: Website: [bytechnology.org](https://bytechnology.org/)

You can also find these in the app under **Settings → About**.

## When you contact support

It helps if you include:

- your phone model and Android version,
- what you were doing and what you expected to happen,
- the time of the event, if it's about a specific alert,
- a screenshot of the Dashboard's **Readiness** tiles, if monitoring isn't working.

!!! danger "In an emergency"
    BY Protect is a support tool, not a replacement for emergency services. It can open your dialer for the police if you add them as a contact, but you still tap Call. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.
