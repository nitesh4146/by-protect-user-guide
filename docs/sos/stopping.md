# Stopping SOS

A full SOS has no cancel countdown. To end it:

1. Open BY Protect. If the screen locked, unlock the phone first.
2. Tap **Stop monitoring**.
3. Confirm with **Stop monitoring**.

This stops the alarm, vibration and torch, and cancels the call if it hasn't been placed yet. Messages already sent can't be recalled. **Let your contacts know you're safe.**

During SOS the notification has no **Stop** button, so a phone that has been taken can't easily end the SOS. Stop it from inside the app.

!!! tip "False alarm?"
    If the app raised SOS by itself and you're fine, stop it and call or text your contacts straight away. The automatic message already tells them it *may be a false alarm*.
