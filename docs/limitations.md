# Limitations and safety notes

BY Protect makes it faster and easier to get help, but it can't guarantee your safety. Please read this page before you rely on it.

## It doesn't contact emergency services

BY Protect only texts and calls **the trusted contacts you add**. It never calls the police, an ambulance or any emergency number by itself. If you're in immediate danger, call your local emergency number yourself. Make sure your contacts know what to do when they receive an alert.

## It only protects you while armed

Detection, recording and every SOS shortcut (the button, the notification, the volume buttons and "help me") work only while monitoring is **armed**.

- **After the phone restarts**, including when the battery runs out, monitoring is **not** turned back on. Open BY Protect and tap **Arm monitoring** again.
- Some phones close background apps to save battery. See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#monitoring-stopped-by-itself).

## Detection isn't perfect

- Cues can be **missed**, for example when the camera is covered, it's dark, the phone is in a bag, or it's noisy, and **false alarms** can happen in busy places. That's why the app combines several cues, and why you can cancel a silent alert.
- **"Help me"** detection may miss the phrase or, rarely, trigger on similar speech.
- The **Speech language monitor** needs an internet connection. Speech is transcribed on the phone, and the transcript can contain mistakes.
- The phone must be **worn facing outward** for the camera to help.

## Messages depend on your phone and network

- SMS and calls need mobile signal and credit. Each alert is longer than one text message, so it's sent as **about two SMS per contact**, at your normal SMS rates.
- On a dual-SIM phone, messages go from the SIM set as the **default for SMS**.
- Location accuracy depends on GPS. Indoors it can be off by tens of metres, or unavailable.

## Your phone's alarm volume

When you arm monitoring, BY Protect sets the phone's **alarm volume to maximum**, so an SOS alarm is always loud. It isn't turned back down when you stop, so **other alarms on your phone, such as your morning alarm clock, will also ring at full volume** until you lower the alarm volume in Android's sound settings.

## Recording other people

While armed, the phone records video, and audio unless you turn **Record audio with video** off, of whoever is around you. Laws on recording people differ between countries and regions. Check what applies where you use the app.

## Your data has no backup

Everything is stored only on this phone. **Uninstalling the app, resetting the phone or moving to a new phone permanently erases** your events, videos, contacts and settings. [Export](events/export-delete.md#export-an-event) anything you need to keep first.
